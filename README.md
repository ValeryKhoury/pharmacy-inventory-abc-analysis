# Inventory Analysis & Policy Design for a Pharmacy

Production Systems II course project, B.E. Industrial Engineering, Lebanese
American University

## Overview
An inventory management study for Germanos Pharmacy. The project used ABC
classification to separate fast- and slow-moving items, then designed and
compared inventory policies to reduce excess stock while keeping a high
service level.

## What I did
- **ABC classification:** analyzed inventory data to classify items by
  movement and value, helping the pharmacy maintain a **98% fill rate**
- **Inventory policy design:** led the development of inventory policies and
  recommended a **continuous review** policy as the best strategy, leading to
  a **14% decrease in excess inventory**

## Methods
- ABC classification
- Continuous review (reorder point / order quantity) policy

## Tools used
Excel, R]

## Notes
Academic project. Item names and quantities have been anonymized. No
